Live career panels · Grades 7 and 8

See the whole path, not just the finish line.

Most students only ever meet the adult at the end of a career. Our panels put three people from the same field in front of a class at once, so students can see every step between where they are now and where a career could take them.

  • 01 · Workplace expertSomeone doing the job today, who knows what the work really looks like.
  • 02 · University studentSomeone studying the field right now, who chose it only a few years ago.
  • 03 · High school studentSomeone a few years ahead of your students, making the same choices they will soon face.
